# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 25, 2026_

This policy explains what the Nootropic Self-Experiment app ("the app", "we") does with your information. The short version: **your experiment data stays on your phone.** We don't run accounts, we don't have a server that stores your results, and we don't sell or share your data for advertising.

## What stays on your device

Everything you enter or measure in the app is stored only in a database on your device:

- the goal and supplement you choose, and your experiment settings (length, blinding mode, check-in window)
- your daily schedule and whether you marked each dose as taken
- your daily test results (reaction times, memory-task scores), your focus, energy and mood ratings, sleep hours, caffeine, notes, and your phone model (used to keep timing comparable)
- your free reaction-test scores
- your verdicts
- any bad reaction you log, and whether you paused, resumed or ended the run

We can't see any of this. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it. We don't back it up.

## Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) and by **RevenueCat**, the service we use to manage subscriptions. To check whether you have an active subscription, RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase and subscription history, and basic device information (such as device type, OS version, and country). It does **not** receive your experiment data or results. See [RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/).

We never see or store your payment card details. Apple or Google processes payments under their own privacy policies.

## Notifications

Daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No reminder content is sent to us.

## Sharing

If you tap a share button, the app creates an image or text with your result and hands it to your phone's share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it, and what happens after that is governed by the app you share to.

## Waitlist (website)

If you join the waitlist on our website, we collect the email address you enter. It is stored by **Formspree**, the form service we use, and used only to email you when Placebo is available. We don't sell it or share it for advertising. To have it removed, contact us (see Contact below) and we'll delete it. See [Formspree's privacy policy](https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy/).

## Analytics and advertising

The app does not include advertising and does not track you across other apps or websites.

We use PostHog for product analytics, to count how many people reach each step: starting onboarding, finishing the quiz, opening the paywall, starting a trial, starting an experiment, finishing the baseline, reaching a verdict and sharing it, and which day of the run you're on when you open the app or log a dose or check-in. Each event carries only the step, a random install ID that isn't linked to your name, email, Apple or Google account, or device ID, your phone's operating system, and in some cases a small number or label such as the day number, the run length (21 or 28 days), the blinding mode, or the subscription plan period.

Analytics never includes your test scores, ratings, notes, Apple Health or Health Connect data, quiz answers, goal, which supplement you're testing, your verdict, or any bad reaction you log. We ask PostHog not to build a profile of you and not to look up your location from your IP address. See [PostHog's privacy policy](https://posthog.com/privacy).

## Health information

The app is a personal measurement tool. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Because your measurements never leave your device, we do not hold any health information about you.

## Children

The app is for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

## Your choices

- You can delete all app data at any time by deleting the app.
- You can cancel a subscription in your App Store or Google Play account settings.
- To ask a question about this policy or about subscription data held by RevenueCat on our behalf, contact us at the address below.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for meaningful changes, tell you in the app.
