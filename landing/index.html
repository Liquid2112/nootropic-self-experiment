How the blind test works

Day 1

Two identical cups

Fill one with your capsule and one with a look-alike, or have a friend do it. The app shuffles which cup you take each day.

Every day

A 2-minute check-in

Tap when the dot appears, hold a few things in mind, rate your mood and energy. Streaks and quests keep you going.

Week 4

The sealed verdict

We unblind your days and compare them properly. You see whether it helped, by how much, and how sure we are.

Questions

Which supplements can I test?

Anything you take as a capsule or powder you can hide in a cup: lion’s mane, L-theanine, creatine, magnesium, and more. Effects that build over weeks get a longer design.

Is it medical advice?

No. Placebo measures how you perform on simple tests. It doesn’t diagnose or treat anything. Talk to a doctor before changing medication.

What does it cost?

The reaction test is free. Running experiments is a subscription with a free trial, and we remind you before the trial ends.
