Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy explains what the Placebo app and the Placebo website ("Placebo", "we") do with your information. Placebo is operated by OPERATOR_NAME_TBD.

The short version: your experiment data and health data stay on your phone. We don't run accounts, we don't have a server that stores your results, and we don't sell your data or share it for advertising.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter or measure in the app is stored only in a database on your device:

  • your quiz answers, the goal and supplement you choose, and your experiment settings (length, blinding mode, check-in window)
  • your daily schedule and whether you marked each dose as taken
  • your daily test results (reaction times, memory and other task scores), your focus, energy, calm and mood ratings, sleep hours, caffeine, notes, and your phone model (used to keep timing comparable)
  • data read from Apple Health or Health Connect, if you connect it (see Health data below)
  • your free reaction-test scores
  • your verdicts
  • any bad reaction you log, and whether you paused, resumed or ended the run
  • your referral code, any code you entered, and the keys you hold for friends

We can't see any of this. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it. We don't back it up.

Health data (Apple Health and Health Connect)

If you connect Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android), the app reads your sleep, resting heart rate and heart rate variability so your experiment can be judged on real data. It never writes to Health.

Health data is used only inside the app, on your device, for your experiment. It is never sent to us or to any other company, never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never included in analytics. You can disconnect at any time in the Health app (or Health Connect) settings.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) and by RevenueCat, the service we use to manage subscriptions. To check whether you have an active subscription, RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase and subscription history, and basic device information (such as device type, OS version, and country). If you use a referral or creator code, RevenueCat also stores your own referral code, the code you entered, and who referred you, so we can credit referral rewards when we offer them and pay creators. It does not receive your experiment data, health data or results. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

We never see or store your payment card details. Apple or Google processes payments under their own privacy policies.

Notifications

Daily reminders and the free-trial reminder are scheduled locally on your device. No reminder content is sent to us.

Sharing and friend keyholders

If you tap a share button, the app creates an image or text with your result and hands it to your phone's share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it, and what happens after that is governed by the app you share to.

If you ask a friend to be your keyholder, the link you send contains what they need to hold your key: the supplement name, the reveal date, the key, which cup is real, and the first name you entered. It travels through the messaging app you choose. We don't store it.

Analytics

The app does not include advertising and does not track you across other apps or websites.

We use PostHog to count how people move through the app, in two groups:

  1. Usage counts (on unless you turn them off): starting onboarding, finishing the quiz, opening the paywall and starting a trial.
  2. Progress counts (off unless you turn them on): starting an experiment, finishing the baseline, which day of the run you're on when you open the app or log a dose or check-in, reaching a verdict and sharing it, and steps in the invite flow (sending or accepting an invite, sending a keyholder a progress update, and sending or receiving an unlock key). You're asked about these on the first screen, and the box starts unchecked.

Each event carries only the step, a random install ID that isn't linked to your name, email, Apple or Google account, or device ID, your phone's operating system, and in some cases a small number or label such as the day number, the run length (21 or 28 days), the blinding mode, the subscription plan period, or how an invite was sent (a link, in person, or a code). Invite events never include your referral code, your friend's name, the key, or which cup is real.

Analytics never includes your test scores, ratings, notes, Apple Health or Health Connect data, quiz answers, goal, which supplement you're testing, your verdict, or any bad reaction you log. We ask PostHog not to build a profile of you and not to look up your location from your IP address. PostHog processes these events for us as our service provider. See PostHog's privacy policy.

Change either setting any time in the app: Journey, then Privacy choices.

Waitlist (website)

If you join the waitlist on our website, we collect the email address you enter. It is stored by Formspree, the form service we use, and used only to email you when Placebo is available. We don't sell it or share it for advertising. We delete it on request, and once the launch emails are sent. See Formspree's privacy policy.

The website also uses PostHog to count page views and waitlist signups. It doesn't set cookies, doesn't record your session, and never sends your email address to PostHog.

Consumer health data (Washington, Nevada, Connecticut and similar laws)

Some state laws, such as Washington's My Health My Data Act, require a separate notice about "consumer health data". Here it is:

  • What we collect: none on our servers. The health-related information described above (test scores, ratings, supplement, bad reactions, Apple Health or Health Connect data) is processed only by the app on your device.
  • Where it comes from: you, your phone's sensors and timers, and Apple Health or Health Connect if you connect them.
  • Why: only to run your experiment and show you your verdict.
  • Who we share it with: no one. We don't sell it. Progress counts (above) are sent only if you opt in, and never include health measurements.
  • Your rights: you can see all of it in the app, delete it by deleting the app, withdraw consent for progress counts at any time in Privacy choices, and contact us with any request (see Contact). We'll answer within 45 days and won't treat you differently for using these rights.

Your privacy rights (US states, EU and UK)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to get a copy, to correct or delete it, to opt out of its sale or use for targeted advertising, and to appeal our decision on your request. We don't sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising, and we don't use it for profiling.

The only personal information we or our service providers hold about you is: the random install ID and the usage events tied to it (PostHog), your purchase records and codes (RevenueCat), and your waitlist email (Formspree). Because the install ID isn't linked to your identity, to act on a request about analytics we may ask you to send the install ID shown in Privacy choices. To make a request, or to appeal a decision, contact us.

If you're in the EU or UK: we process analytics on the basis of our legitimate interest in fixing and improving the app (usage counts) or your consent (progress counts), purchase data to perform our contract with you, and your waitlist email on the basis of your consent. You can complain to your local data protection authority.

Retention and security

Analytics events are kept for up to 2 years, then deleted. Purchase records are kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat as long as needed for your subscription and the law. Waitlist emails are deleted after launch or on request. Data on your device is protected by your phone's own security and is deleted with the app.

Children

Placebo is for adults 18 and older, and the app asks you to confirm your age before you start. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think a minor has used Placebo, contact us and we'll delete anything we hold.

Health information disclaimer

The app is a personal measurement tool. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for meaningful changes, tell you in the app before the change applies to you.
