Support

Need help with Placebo? Email support@placeboapp.com and a real person will answer, usually within two business days.

Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription? On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Placebo, and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before your trial or billing period ends to avoid the next charge. We can't cancel or refund App Store purchases for you; Apple handles refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I bought Pro but the app doesn't show it. On the paywall, tap Restore. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with.

Where is my data? Your experiments, test scores and Health data stay on your phone. We don't have accounts or a server that stores them. Deleting the app deletes them. See the privacy policy.

I felt unwell during an experiment. Stop taking the supplement and contact a doctor. In an emergency call 911. In the app, tap "Feeling unwell? Log a bad reaction" on Today to pause your run. Placebo is not medical advice.

Can I change my supplement or my tests mid-run? No, that would weaken the verdict. End the run and start a new one.
