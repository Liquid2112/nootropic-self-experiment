# Stack City renderer: build brief

Build a reusable, JSON-driven renderer for short vertical "Stack City" TikTok videos (1080x1920, 30fps). A working one-off prototype is in prototype/ (scene.html + render.js; serve this folder over http, e.g. python -m http.server, then node prototype/render.js http://localhost:8000/prototype/scene.html frames 9 30); generalize it. Keep the look exactly: blocky low-poly flat-shaded characters, dark navy/indigo night backdrop with tangerine/lilac/blue bokeh, faint glowing grid floor, black chapter "pill" at top, Inter Black word-by-word captions (white, thick black stroke, highlighted words in tangerine #FF5A36). Brand: tang #FF5A36, lilac #B7A6FF, ink #15131C, paper #F7F4EE. All designs are original (never Roblox/Minecraft assets or logos).

DELIVERABLE: a folder `stack-city-renderer/` (Node, three@0.170.0, playwright, ffmpeg on PATH):
1. `engine/scene.html` + modules: loads an episode JSON, exposes window.renderAt(t) deterministically (no real-time clocks or Math.random; seeded RNG only).
2. Character builders (box primitives like the prototype, with talk/blink/head-bob/point/thumbs-up/flex poses): Brian (white lab coat, cube head split tangerine/lilac, tie), Benny (brown coffee-bean hoodie, paper cup on head), Tea (green hoodie, leaf sprout on head), Shroom (shaggy cream hoodie, hood up, sleepy eyes), Brick (tall gym bro, capsule-striped tank top).
3. Prop library: supplement jars (slam-in), two mugs (cup shuffle), phone card, calendar card (days flip to tang/lilac then grey "blinded"), reaction button + ms counter, verdict card (ON vs OFF bars + KEEP IT stamp, values from JSON), line chart that draws itself, coins burst, confetti blocks, boxing ring, scoreboard, poll card. 2D cards are canvas textures on planes.
4. Episode JSON schema (document it in README): duration, optional voiceover audio path, beats[] each with {t0,t1,pill,caption words with highlight markers, cast positions+actions, props+animations, camera preset: punch_in | medium | wide | orbit | pov, with lerp/ease}. Caption words can be timed automatically (even spacing) or from a word-timestamps JSON.
5. `render.js episodes/X.json out.mp4`: Playwright headless (chromium flags --use-gl=angle --use-angle=swiftshader --enable-unsafe-swiftshader), deviceScaleFactor 2 at 540x960 viewport, screenshot per frame, ffmpeg encode libx264 crf 19 yuv420p, mux voiceover + loudnorm I=-14 if given. Also a `--stills t1,t2,...` mode for quick contact sheets.
6. `episodes/ep1_prove_it.json` implementing the test script below, and render it once to confirm everything works (silent is fine). This is a test fixture only, it will not be posted.
