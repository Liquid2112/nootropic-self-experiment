# Age rating

Recommended result: **18+**, matching the privacy policy ("The app is for
people 18 and older") and the business plan's audience.

Apple's questionnaire (revised in 2025 with 13+, 16+ and 18+ tiers) is answered
honestly, then the rating is raised to 18+ with the "override to a higher
rating" option at the end. Answer it truthfully even though we override: the
answers show on the product page and inconsistent answers are their own
rejection reason.

## Answers

**Features / capabilities**

| Question | Answer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Parental controls | No | |
| Age assurance | No | |
| Unrestricted web access | No | Links open specific pages (PubMed, store) in the system browser; there's no in-app browser |
| User-generated content | No | Nothing a user types is shown to anyone else |
| Messaging and chat | No | |
| Advertising | No | Affiliate links are not ads served by an ad network |

**Mature themes**

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Profanity or crude humor | None |
| Horror or fear themes | None |
| Alcohol, tobacco or drug use or references | **Infrequent/Mild** (library mentions nicotine gum, caffeine and prescription drugs like modafinil; becomes **Frequent** if the gray-market section ships, see app-review.md #2) |
| Mature or suggestive themes | None |

**Medical or wellness**

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Medical or treatment information | **Infrequent/Mild** (educational supplement profiles, safety notes, no diagnosis or dosing) |
| Health or wellness topics | **Yes** |

**Sexuality / nudity, violence, chance-based activities**: all None / No.
(No gambling, no loot boxes, no contests.)

Apple computes something around 13+ or 16+ from these answers. Then set the
**age rating override to 18+**.

## Why 18+ and not the computed rating

- Supplements and prescription stimulants aren't for minors, and the library
  talks about both.
- The privacy policy already says 18+, and the rating should match it.
- It costs us nothing: the target audience is college students and adults, and
  TikTok traffic under 18 would be users we shouldn't have anyway.
